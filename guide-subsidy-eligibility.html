Which electric scooters actually qualify for the PM E-DRIVE subsidy?

We ran all 44 electric scooters in our database against the scheme rules. 4 get nothing at all, 3 get less than the full amount, and the rest all get exactly the same ₹5,000.

✍️ ScooterWale Editorial
📅 Verified 17 October 2026
⏱ 7 min read

💡 The short answer

PM E-DRIVE pays ₹2,500 per kWh, capped at ₹5,000, only on scooters with an ex-factory price at or below ₹1.5 lakh. Because the cap is reached at a 2 kWh battery, 37 of our 44 models receive exactly ₹5,000, 3 receive less because their packs are small, and 4 receive nothing because they are too expensive.

🧮 Why battery size mostly does not matter

At ₹2,500 per kWh, a 2 kWh battery already produces ₹5,000, which is the cap. Every scooter above 2 kWh therefore receives the identical ₹5,000. A 5.2 kWh Ola S1 Pro+ would receive the same as a 2 kWh Ola S1 X, if the Pro+ were eligible at all. The per-kWh framing that dealers and manufacturers use makes the scheme sound more generous on large batteries than it is.

⚠️ How we calculate ex-factory, and why it is an estimate

The ₹1.5 lakh eligibility test uses ex-factory price, which manufacturers do not publish. We derive it from ex-showroom price by removing the 5% GST that applies to electric vehicles. Real ex-factory also excludes dealer margin, so true figures are a little lower than ours and the models we show near the line may in fact clear it comfortably. Treat this table as a strong guide, and confirm the ex-factory figure on your invoice before assuming a subsidy. Where a model's status is genuinely borderline, ask the dealer to show you its Ministry of Heavy Industries empanelment.

The other eligibility conditions

Price is the rule that excludes the most models, but it is not the only one. To qualify a scooter must also:

  • Be fitted with an advanced battery as defined by the scheme
  • Come from a manufacturer empanelled with the Ministry of Heavy Industries
  • Be sold through a dealer registered on the PM E-DRIVE and VAHAN portals
  • Be registered in India, privately or corporately owned, both of which are eligible

The subsidy is also limited to one two-wheeler claim per Aadhaar, and you must apply within 30 days of your RC being generated. Full process on the how to claim page.

Common questions

How much PM E-DRIVE subsidy do I get on an electric scooter?

₹5,000, if your scooter has a battery of 2 kWh or larger and an ex-factory price at or below ₹1.5 lakh. Below 2 kWh you get ₹2,500 per kWh. Above ₹1.5 lakh ex-factory you get nothing.

Why does the Ather 450X get no subsidy?

Its ex-factory price is above the ₹1.5 lakh scheme ceiling. The same applies to the Ather 450 Apex, the Ola S1 Pro+ 5.2 kWh and the TVS iQube ST 5.3 kWh. PM E-DRIVE is designed for mass-market adoption, so the premium segment is deliberately excluded.

Does a bigger battery get a bigger subsidy?

No. The ₹5,000 cap is reached at 2 kWh, so every eligible scooter above that gets the same amount.

Which is the cheapest scooter that gets the full ₹5,000?

The Bounce Infinity Infinity E1 2.0 kWh at ₹68,999 ex-showroom, with a 2.0 kWh pack.
