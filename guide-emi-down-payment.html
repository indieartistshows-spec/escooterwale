Electric scooter down payment and EMI: what you will pay each month

Most electric scooters are bought on a loan. Here is what the monthly EMI looks like with different down payments, which subsidies reduce the loan and which only come back later, and what to check before you sign.

EScooterwale Editorial
Verified 16 November 2026
3 min read

The short answer

EMI on a popular electric scooter starts from about ₹2,370 a month with no down payment. Putting down ₹10,000 cuts it by about ₹260 a month, and 20% down cuts it by a fifth. Prices updated 16 November 2026.

How much down payment do you need?

Many dealers and brand finance partners offer zero or low down payment schemes, but they usually come with a processing fee and a higher interest rate. A down payment of 10 to 20% of the price is common and keeps both the EMI and the total interest lower.

Remember that the on-road price is a little higher than ex-showroom. Insurance and handling are often paid upfront, so add them to the cash you need on the day.

Which subsidies lower the loan

The central PM E-DRIVE subsidy is taken off the invoice at the dealer, so it reduces the amount you borrow. It is up to ₹5,000 on eligible scooters.

State subsidies such as Delhi's are usually paid into your bank account after you register the scooter. They do not reduce your down payment or the loan, so do not count them when you plan the first payment. Our subsidy guide lists what each state pays and when.

Before you sign

  • Ask for the interest rate as a yearly rate, not a flat rate. A flat rate looks lower but costs more.
  • Ask for the processing fee and any charge for paying off early.
  • Compare the dealer's finance with your own bank. A personal or two-wheeler loan from your bank can be cheaper.
  • Keep the loan short if you can. A longer tenure lowers the EMI but raises the total interest.

Common questions

What is the down payment for an electric scooter?

Many dealers offer low or zero down payment schemes, but 10 to 20% of the price is common. Insurance and handling are often paid upfront as well.

What is the EMI of an electric scooter?

From about ₹2,370 a month for the Ola S1 X 2 kWh with no down payment, over 48 months at 12% a year. Popular models between ₹1 lakh and ₹1.6 lakh cost roughly ₹2,600 to ₹4,200 a month.

Does the subsidy reduce my EMI?

The central PM E-DRIVE subsidy does, because it comes off the invoice. State subsidies are usually paid back to you after registration, so they do not reduce the loan.
