Which electric scooters use LFP batteries, and which use NMC?

We checked cell chemistry for all 38 models against manufacturer spec sheets and trade press. 5 confirmed LFP, 20 confirmed NMC, and 12 where the brand simply will not say.

EScooterwale Editorial
Verified 26 October 2026
6 min read

The short answer

Of the 38 scooters we track, 20 use NMC, 5 use LFP, 1 may be either depending on supply, and 12 come from brands that do not publish the chemistry at all. LFP handles Indian heat better and lasts more cycles. NMC packs more range into the same space. Ampere and BGauss are the LFP brands. Ola, Ather, TVS, Okinawa and Bounce are NMC.

The LFP scooters

LFP, or lithium iron phosphate, runs cooler, tolerates heat better and typically survives more charge cycles than NMC. It is also heavier and less energy dense for the same capacity, which is why it tends to appear on value-focused models rather than performance ones. Notably, the LFP brands also offer the longest battery warranties on this list.

  • Ampere Magnus Neo 2.3 kWh — 2.3 kWh, 5 yr / 75,000 km
  • Ampere Magnus G Max 3 kWh — 3 kWh, 5 yr / 75,000 km
  • Bgauss C12i EX 2.0 kWh — 2 kWh, 3 yr / 36,000 km
  • Ampere Nexus EX+ 3 kWh — 3 kWh, 5 yr / 75,000 km
  • Bgauss RUV 350 MAX 3.0 kWh — 3 kWh, 3 yr battery and motor

Ather began fitting LFP to the smaller Rizta pack in July 2026, its first use of the chemistry. Which one you get depends on supply and cannot be specified when ordering.

  • Ather Energy Rizta S 2.9 kWh — 2.9 kWh, 5 yr / 60,000 km

The NMC scooters

NMC, or nickel manganese cobalt, is more energy dense, so it delivers more range per kilogram and per litre of pack volume. It is more sensitive to sustained heat, which places more weight on the quality of the battery management and thermal design. Every scooter with a claimed range above 200 km on this list is NMC.

Bajaj is the most notable. Its FAQs say only "advanced chemistry Lithium Ion cells sourced from reputed manufacturers". Plenty of websites will confidently tell you the Chetak uses NMC. If chemistry matters to your purchase, ask the dealer to put the answer in writing on the invoice.

Which chemistry should you actually pick?

Chemistry is a weaker signal than most buying guides suggest. A well-engineered NMC pack with proper thermal management will outlast a badly managed LFP one. What we would actually weight, in order:

  • Warranty length. Ampere's 5 years and 75,000 km is the strongest on this list and tells you more than the chemistry label does
  • Whether you park in direct sun. If you do, LFP has a real advantage in an Indian summer
  • Whether you need to carry the battery inside. That rules out most of the range immediately
  • Chemistry itself, last

Common questions

What battery type does the Bajaj Chetak use, NMC or LFP?

Bajaj does not say, for any Chetak variant. Its own FAQs describe only "advanced chemistry Lithium Ion cells". Sites that state NMC are repeating an unsourced claim.

What battery type does the TVS iQube use?

NMC. TVS Motor states NMC cell chemistry across the 2.2 kWh, 3.5 kWh and 5.3 kWh iQube variants.

What battery type does Hero Vida use?

Not published. Hero markets the removable pack rather than the chemistry. All three Vida models on sale have removable batteries.

Does Ola use LFP or NMC?

NMC throughout. Ola used LG 21700 NMC cells and is transitioning to its in-house 4680 Bharat Cell, which is also NMC. That is a change of cell format, not chemistry.

Which lasts longer in Indian heat?

LFP, other things being equal. It degrades more slowly at high ambient temperatures and tolerates more charge cycles. Other things are rarely equal though, so read the warranty as well as the chemistry.

How we verify this page

Chemistry is taken from manufacturer specification sheets where published, and from Autocar India, Autocar Professional, EVreporter or Rushlane where the manufacturer is silent but credible technical coverage exists. Where neither is available we print "Not published" rather than inferring. Okinawa's NMC entry is a brand-level statement rather than a per-model spec line, and TVS's NMC confirmation comes from TVS Motor's own FAQ. Last verified 26 October 2026.
