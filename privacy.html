Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

This policy explains how EScooterwale ("we", "us") handles personal data collected through escooterwale.in, including the scooter match quiz at /match. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules made under it.

In short: if you fill in our form, we keep your name, mobile number, email, pincode and scooter choices. With your consent we share them with partner scooter brands and their dealers so they can send you prices and test ride offers, and we may send you the EScooterwale newsletter. You can ask us to stop or to delete your data at any time by writing to info@escooterwale.in.

1. What we collect

  • Details you give us: name, mobile number, email address and pincode, when you submit a form such as "Save my shortlist".
  • Your scooter preferences: your quiz answers (budget, daily distance, who rides, charging, priority, licence), the scooters you shortlisted and the ones you skipped.
  • Technical details: the page you submitted from, the site language, how you reached us (for example a search engine or a campaign link) and whether you used a phone or a computer.
  • Analytics: we use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand how the site is used, such as pages visited and buttons clicked. These tools use cookies. We never send your name, number or email to them.

We do not collect payment details, government ID numbers or sensitive personal data.

2. Why we use it

  • To show you scooters that match your needs and on-road prices for your area.
  • To send you your shortlist and price information you asked for.
  • To pass your details to partner brands and dealers, if you consented, so they can contact you about prices, test rides and offers.
  • To send you the EScooterwale newsletter, including content and offers made with scooter brands, if you consented.
  • To understand, in aggregate, what buyers look for, so we can improve the site and our content.
  • To keep the site secure and stop spam or misuse.

3. Your consent

We only collect your details through a form when you tick the consent box yourself. The box is never ticked in advance. The exact wording you agreed to is saved with your details. You can withdraw consent at any time (see section 7). Withdrawing does not affect anything done before you withdrew.

4. Who we share it with

  • Partner scooter brands and their authorised dealers: electric scooter makers listed on EScooterwale with whom we have an arrangement, and dealers they authorise. We share only what they need to contact you: your name, mobile number, email, pincode or city and the scooters you are interested in. Once they receive your details, the brand or dealer is also responsible for handling them under the law.
  • Service providers who run the site for us: hosting (Vercel), storage of form entries (Google Workspace) and analytics (Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity). They process data only on our instructions.
  • Authorities: if the law requires us to.

We do not sell your data to anyone other than partner scooter brands and their dealers as described above, and never without your consent.

5. How long we keep it

We keep form entries for up to 12 months from the date you submitted them, or until you ask us to delete them, whichever comes first. After that we delete them or keep only figures that cannot identify you.

6. How we protect it

Form entries are sent over an encrypted connection and stored in a private spreadsheet that only authorised people can open. Access keys are kept in protected settings, not in the website code. No system is completely secure, but if a breach that affects you ever happens, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and who we shared it with,
  • correct or update it,
  • delete it,
  • stop sending you the newsletter or stop sharing your details with partners (withdraw consent).

Write to info@escooterwale.in from the email you used, or include the mobile number you gave us, so we can find your entry. Every newsletter also has an unsubscribe link. We aim to reply within 7 days and to act on your request within 30 days. When you ask us to delete your data, we also tell the partners we shared it with.

8. Grievances

For any complaint about how we handle your data, contact our grievance contact at info@escooterwale.in with the subject "Privacy". If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Children

Our forms are meant for adults buying a vehicle. Please do not submit them if you are under 18.

10. Cookies

We use cookies for analytics and to remember small settings such as your chosen language. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site still works without them.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. If we change how we share your data, we will ask for your consent again before doing so.
