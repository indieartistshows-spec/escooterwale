Electric scooter on-road price: what is included in each state

The on-road price is what you actually pay to ride the scooter home. For electric scooters it is much closer to the ex-showroom price than for petrol, but it still varies by state. Here is how it adds up.

EScooterwale Editorial
Verified 23 November 2026
3 min read

The short answer

On-road price = ex-showroom price + road tax + registration + insurance + number plate and handling. For electric scooters, registration is free everywhere and most states charge no road tax, so on-road is usually only 5 to 6% above ex-showroom.

What goes into the on-road price

  • Ex-showroom price. The price set by the brand, including 5% GST on electric vehicles. The central PM E-DRIVE subsidy is taken off this at the dealer on eligible models.
  • Road tax. A one time state tax. Most states charge nothing on electric scooters; the table below shows each state.
  • Registration. Free for battery operated vehicles across India.
  • Insurance. First year comprehensive plus multi year third party cover is compulsory. You can buy it from any insurer, not only the one the dealer offers.
  • Number plate and handling. The high security number plate and the dealer's paperwork. Ask for these as separate lines.

How to check an on-road quote

  • Ask for an itemised quote, with every line named.
  • Check the PM E-DRIVE subsidy is shown as a deduction if the model is eligible.
  • Question any "logistics", "handling" or "accessories" charge you did not ask for.
  • Get a second insurance quote online before you accept the dealer's.

Common questions

What is included in the on-road price of an electric scooter?

The ex-showroom price, road tax (zero in most states), registration (free for EVs), first year insurance, and the number plate and handling charges.

Is road tax charged on electric scooters?

Most states charge no road tax on electric scooters. A few charge a percentage or have not confirmed their rule; the state table on this page shows each one.

Can I buy insurance outside the dealer?

Yes. You can buy the compulsory insurance from any insurer and give the policy to the dealer before registration, which is often cheaper.
