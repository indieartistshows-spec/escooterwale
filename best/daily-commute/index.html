Daily Commute: Our Top 5 Picks

For daily commuters, three things matter above everything: real-world range (not IDC claims), charging convenience (can you charge at home overnight?), and service reliability (will it be available every morning?). This list prioritises all three.

For most Indian daily commuters, the Ather 450X Gen 3 is the clear answer — if you're in an Ather service city. The ride quality, reliability, and charging network make every morning stress-free.
