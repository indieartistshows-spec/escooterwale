Key EMI tips
Put down 20–30% to significantly reduce monthly EMI and total interest.
24-month tenure has higher EMI but saves ₹8,000–12,000 in interest vs 48 months.
EV-specific loans from HDFC, SBI, and Bajaj Finance offer 7.5–9% rates — lower than standard auto loans.
FAME-III subsidy of ₹10,000–15,000 reduces your effective ex-showroom price before EMI calculation.
Pre-approved loans from banks may offer better rates than in-house dealer financing.
