Is the Chetak battery LFP or NMC?

Bajaj does not publish the cell chemistry. Its FAQs only say the Chetak uses advanced chemistry lithium-ion cells. What you can count on is the 3 year or 50,000 km battery warranty, and the real range we list for each variant.

Which Chetak should I buy?

If you want navigation and keyless start, the 3501. If you want the same battery and range for less, the 3503 is the value pick. The 3502 sits in between with a TFT screen and a front disc brake.
