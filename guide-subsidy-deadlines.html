Electric scooter subsidy last date: every deadline that matters in 2026

The central scheme runs to March 2028. Several state schemes end much sooner. This page tracks every date and is re-checked every week.

💡 The short answer

The central PM E-DRIVE scheme runs to 31 March 2028, so there is no rush on the central ₹5,000. The dates that matter are at state level, soonest first: Odisha 31 Dec 2026, Telangana 31 Dec 2026, Rajasthan ~30 Sep 2027.

Central deadline: 31 March 2028

PM E-DRIVE support for electric two-wheelers was due to lapse in 2026. The Ministry of Heavy Industries extended it to 31 March 2028 in August 2026, with a bigger budget. Claims have to be filed by 31 December 2027.

The rate itself did not change. It was halved from ₹5,000/kWh to ₹2,500/kWh on 1 April 2025 and has stayed there. The cap remains ₹5,000 per scooter.

Is there a separate deadline for Chetak, iQube, Ola or Ather?

No. Subsidy deadlines belong to the government scheme, not to the brand. A Bajaj Chetak buyer, a TVS iQube buyer and an Ather buyer all work to the same central date of 31 March 2028, plus whatever state deadline applies where the scooter is registered.

If a dealer tells you the "Chetak subsidy ends this month", they are describing a manufacturer discount or a sales target, not a government scheme. Ask them to name the notification.

What can change by brand is eligibility. If a manufacturer raises a model's price above ₹1.5 lakh ex-factory, that model stops qualifying from the date of the price change. We track which models sit close to that line on our eligibility page.

⏳ Delhi tapers, so the real deadline is sooner than 2030

Delhi's EV Policy 2026 was notified on 1 July 2026 and runs to March 2030, but the two-wheeler incentive drops every year:

  • Year 1 (to roughly 30 June 2027): ₹10,000/kWh, capped at ₹30,000
  • Year 2: ₹6,600/kWh, capped at ₹20,000
  • Year 3: ₹3,300/kWh, capped at ₹10,000

A Delhi buyer who waits twelve months loses ₹10,000. That is the deadline that actually costs money.

Common questions

What is the last date for the electric scooter subsidy in India?

31 March 2028 for the central PM E-DRIVE scheme. State deadlines are earlier and vary: Odisha 31 Dec 2026, Telangana 31 Dec 2026, Rajasthan ~30 Sep 2027.

What is the Odisha EV subsidy last date?

The Commerce and Transport Department extended the Odisha EV Policy 2021 to 31 December 2026 (reported 5 October 2026). This is the third extension, after 31 March and 30 September 2026. The state pays 15% of ex-factory price capped at ₹5,000.

Will PM E-DRIVE be extended again beyond 2028?

Nobody knows yet. The scheme has been extended and the two-wheeler budget raised by about 85%, which suggests continued support. But the rate was also halved in April 2025, so the amount per scooter is heading down even as the timeline stretches.

What happens if the scheme ends before my scooter is delivered?

Eligibility is normally locked at booking and advance payment, not at delivery. Book before the deadline and you should keep the subsidy even if delivery slips. Get it in writing.

Should I wait for a better subsidy?

On the central scheme, no. The rate has already halved once and there is no sign of an increase. In Delhi, waiting costs you money because the incentive tapers each year. The only reason to wait is if your state is about to announce a new policy, which currently applies to Uttarakhand, Goa and Assam.
