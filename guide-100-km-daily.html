Best electric scooter for 100 km a day in 2026

A claimed range of 150 km does not mean 100 km a day is easy. Here is what you need, and the scooters that actually manage it on one charge, using our real range figures instead of the brochure.

EScooterwale Editorial

3 min read

The short answer

For 100 km a day, buy a scooter whose real range starts at 120 km or more. That leaves a 20% reserve for hills, a pillion, hot days and an older battery. The cheapest that clears it is the Ola S1 X 3 kWh at ₹1,03,999. With the widest service network, the TVS iQube ST 5.3 kWh at ₹1,78,877.

Why claimed range is not enough

Brands quote the IDC range, measured on a test cycle at low speed with one light rider. On real roads, with traffic, a pillion now and then and speeds of 50 to 60 km/h, our measured range is usually 20 to 30% lower. A scooter claiming 150 km often gives you 110 to 120 km.

Battery packs also lose some capacity over the years, and you will not want to arrive home on the last 5%. So for a daily 100 km, we look for real range that starts at 120 km or more.

Our picks for 100 km a day

Best value: Ola S1 X 4 kWh

179 to 203 km of real range for ₹1,19,999, which is the most range per rupee among scooters from the big brands. It qualifies for ₹5,000 of PM E-DRIVE subsidy. Check service in your area first: Ola is moving from its own stores to dealers.

Best service network: TVS iQube ST 5.3 kWh

155 to 175 km of real range and a full home charge in about 4 hrs. TVS has one of the widest EV service networks in India, which matters when you ride this much. It costs ₹1,78,877 and gets no PM E-DRIVE subsidy because it is above the price cap.

Removable battery: Hero Vida VX2 Plus 4.4 kWh

138 to 157 km of real range and a removable battery you can charge indoors, at ₹1,60,990. Hero also has a large service network, which helps outside the big cities.

Cheapest that clears 120 km: Ola S1 X 3 kWh

130 to 145 km for ₹1,03,999. It clears our bar with little to spare, so it suits a flat daily 100 km better than a hilly one.

Charging every night

At 100 km a day you will charge every night. Look at the home charging time in the table: anything under 5 hours fits easily overnight. A 15 A socket on its own circuit is the safe choice; ask the dealer to check your wiring at delivery.

What 100 km a day costs to run

An electric scooter uses roughly 2 to 3 units of electricity per 100 km. At ₹8 a unit that is about ₹16 to ₹24 a day, against roughly ₹200 a day in petrol for a scooter doing 50 km per litre at ₹100 a litre. Over a year of 300 riding days, that is a saving of more than ₹50,000, which is why high mileage riders gain the most from going electric.

Common questions

Which electric scooter can do 100 km a day?

Any scooter whose real range starts at 120 km or more, such as the Ola S1 X 4 kWh (179 to 203 km), TVS iQube ST 5.3 kWh (155 to 175 km) or Hero Vida VX2 Plus 4.4 kWh (138 to 157 km).

Is a 100 km claimed range scooter enough for 100 km a day?

No. Real range is usually 20 to 30% below the claimed figure, so a scooter claiming 100 km gives you about 70 to 80 km in daily use.

What is the cheapest electric scooter for 100 km a day?

The Ola S1 X 3 kWh at ₹1,03,999 ex-showroom, with 130 to 145 km of real range.
