Ather Konarc launched from ₹99,999

Ather's first scooter on its new EL platform, shown at Community Day. It is a family scooter with a steel unibody, metal panels and IP67 sealing, priced well below the Rizta.

Ampere

Ampere Reo VYB launched from ₹69,499

A low-speed city scooter with an LFP battery that Ampere rates for up to 1 lakh km. At 25 km/h it needs no licence or registration, which makes it an easy first EV for students and short errands.

  • Launched at ₹69,499 introductory, now ₹71,999 on Ampere's site
  • 80 km real riding range claimed
  • LFP battery, rated up to 1 lakh km
  • 24 L boot, keyless start, front disc
  • 3 years or 30,000 km warranty
