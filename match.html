How to choose the right electric scooter to buy

Most people pick an EV scooter from an ad or a friend's advice and find out later that the range, the boot or the service centre does not fit their life. These six things decide whether a scooter suits you. The match quiz above weighs all of them.

1. Real range for your day

Brands quote the IDC test range. On Indian roads you usually get 20% to 35% less. Pick a scooter whose real range covers your daily ride with room to spare. See our real-world range tests.

2. On-road price, not just ex-showroom

Road tax, insurance and handling are added on top, and the PM E-DRIVE subsidy and some state schemes come off. Check the subsidy in your state and work out the EMI.

3. Where you will charge

With your own parking spot, any scooter works. Without one, look for more range so you charge fewer times a week, or a removable battery.

4. Service centre near you

An EV needs little maintenance, but when it does, distance to the service centre matters more than any feature. Compare brand service networks.

5. Battery type and warranty

LFP batteries last more charge cycles, NMC packs are lighter and hold more energy. Check the battery warranty in years and kilometres. LFP vs NMC explained.

6. Licence or low-speed

Scooters capped at 25 km/h need no licence or registration. Everything faster needs both.

Questions buyers ask

Which electric scooter should I buy in India?

The right one depends on your budget, how far you ride each day, who rides with you and where you can charge. Start from your real daily distance and pick a scooter whose real-world range covers it with at least 30% to spare, from a brand with a service centre near you. The match quiz above does this for you in 7 questions.

How much range do I need in an electric scooter?

Plan on real-world range, not the claimed IDC figure. Real range is usually 20% to 35% lower than the claim. If you ride 40 km a day, look for at least 55 km of real range so you are not charging to the last percent.

What is the best electric scooter under ₹1 lakh?

Under ₹1 lakh ex-showroom you get practical city scooters with 2 to 3 kWh batteries. See our ranked list of the best electric scooters under ₹1 lakh, or take the quiz with the under ₹1 lakh budget to see the ones that fit your daily distance.

Do I need a licence for an electric scooter?

Yes for most models. Electric scooters with a top speed above 25 km/h need a driving licence and registration. Low-speed scooters capped at 25 km/h need neither.

Is the PM E-DRIVE subsidy still available on electric scooters?

Yes. PM E-DRIVE gives ₹2,500 per kWh of battery, capped at ₹5,000 per scooter, and runs until 31 March 2028. The dealer deducts it from the price at the time of sale. Some states add their own subsidy or road tax waiver. See the subsidy in your state.

Should I buy an electric scooter online or from a dealer?

You can book many models online, but take a test ride at a dealer first, check the on-road price in writing and confirm the nearest service centre. Delivery, registration and the subsidy are handled by the dealer either way.
