Electric scooters in India 2026: how to choose

There is now an electric scooter for almost every budget in India, from the Ampere Reo at under ₹60,000 to the Ola S1 Pro+ and Ather 450 Apex at the top. TVS and Bajaj lead sales, Ather leads on ride quality, and Ola goes furthest on a charge for the money.

The key factors to evaluate: real-world range (not IDC claims), service network coverage in your city, charging convenience at home or nearby, and total on-road cost including insurance, road tax in your state and the PM E-DRIVE subsidy.
